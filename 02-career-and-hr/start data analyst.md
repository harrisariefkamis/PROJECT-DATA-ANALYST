## Choosing a Role (Guidance)
- Answer data requirement and enablement in a company.
- Interest in building data infrastructure: consider Data Engineer / Data Architect.
- Interest in research and new tech: consider Data Scientist / ML roles.
- Interest in deep analysis, anomaly detection, pattern discovery: consider Data Analyst / Researcher.

Consider your preference, not only salary. Think about project scope, duration, and problem types you enjoy.

## Bonus — Why I shifted into Data Analyst? (Personal experience)
- Demand for industrial engineers decreased while demand for data roles increased (since 2018)

Ensure your driver is personal and sustainable (financial, family time, curiosity). Avoid following trends alone.
